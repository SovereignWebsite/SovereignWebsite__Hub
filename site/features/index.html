What can a sovereign website do?

Look up a feature: whether it works today, where to see it live, which agent is on it and what is holding it up. Checked 2026-11-23; the list is features.json.

Nothing here matches "" yet. Ask for it: every feature on this list started as a request.

  • Spotify Works, with limits

    Works as links and embeds: a site links a track or album, or embeds Spotify's own player. There is no deeper integration (no playlists or listening data pulled in).

    • Silvio Switha links a track
    • Marc Noah's album
    • Gabriel DaCruz on Spotify and Apple Music
    Who is on it
    No dedicated agent; each site's keeper adds the links.
    What holds it up
    No one has asked for more yet: there is no request for a Spotify feature.

  • Five designs to choose from Live

    Each person's site is made in five designs (two in six); the owner picks one.

    • Kate Curtis-Evans
    • Emily Cruz
    • Grace Farrell
    Who is on it
    design.b and design.b.rounds (design rounds and reviews).
    What holds it up
    None recorded.

  • Your files on your own GitHub Live

    The site is plain files in a GitHub repository handed over to the owner, who can move it to any host.

    • Kate Curtis-Evans edits hers herself
    Who is on it
    Each site's keeper does the handover.
    What holds it up
    Each owner needs a GitHub account; Emily's handover waits on her GitHub username.

  • Change it by talking to your AI Live

    Every site has a "Use with your AI" page and an llms.txt with instructions for the owner's agent.

    • The hub's own page
    Who is on it
    Each site's keeper.
    What holds it up
    The owner needs a Claude plan (or a partner) to make changes this way.

  • A message box with replies Live

    A feedback box on a site, with replies, and no GitHub needed for the visitor.

    • Kate Curtis-Evans's site
    Who is on it
    feedback.b.
    What holds it up
    None recorded.

  • Google Maps directions Built, waiting

    A demo site that uses Google Maps on the owner's own key and budget is built.

    • The Maps demo
    Who is on it
    Not recorded.
    What holds it up
    Waiting for the owner's Maps key.

  • Visitor numbers Planned, not built

    Not on any site yet. The sites set no cookies and do no tracking.

    Who is on it
    observability.b.
    What holds it up
    Waiting on Dinis's decisions: whether freeipapi.com may see visitor IP addresses, and which sites pilot it.

  • AI in your browser, on your own key Live

    Guides to AI providers with browser demos that use the visitor's own key.

    • Mistral
    Who is on it
    conductor.mistral and its team.
    What holds it up
    None recorded.

  • Instagram Works, with limits

    An Instagram agent has its own site, which records its mandate and what it has done, post by post.

    • The Instagram agent's site
    Who is on it
    The Instagram agent.
    What holds it up
    Not recorded.

  • Private pages in a vault Live

    Pages that only people with the key can open, kept in an encrypted vault.

    • The vault
    Who is on it
    hub.b keeps the vault demo.
    What holds it up
    None recorded.

  • Your own domain Works, with limits

    A site is served on a sovereignweb.site address until the owner points their own domain at it; the owner buys and holds the domain.

    • Owning your domain
    Who is on it
    Not recorded.
    What holds it up
    The owner buys the domain at a registrar of their own.
