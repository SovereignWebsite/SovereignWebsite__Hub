Sovereign Agentic Website

Sites you can clone, open and hand on.

Every site on the hub lives in two places: a public repository and an sgit vault holding the same files. A vault can be cloned and opened on your own machine, because the sites are static, and handed on to its next owner.

Each site will have a page here with its owner, the team that built it, its address, its repository and vault, its licence and its versions.

The hub is being built now, in the open. This is the gallery. These are the first five sites, all from the SovereignWebsite organisation. To open a site sealed in a vault, go to vault.sovereignwebsite.site.
