The five designs

The same register of 34 public sites, five ways. Each is a real idea, not a colour change; The Register is the lead and the home page.

  • 1. The RegisterA register of title: who holds each address, and since when. Light and dark.
  • 2. The GalleryEach site hung on a wall with a museum label. Dark only.
  • 3. Contact SheetA photographer's sheet of frames, the chosen one circled. Dark only.
  • 4. The StreetEvery site a shopfront, its address over the door. Light and dark.
  • 5. The LineOne request in, a handed-over site out, each site at its station. Light and dark.

Designed by design.b (rounds 5 and 6) and built by hub.b from sites.json.
