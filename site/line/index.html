What you get

  1. A site about you, at its own address

    Your work, your story and how to reach you, made from your CV, photos and links.

    See it: Emily Cruz's site

  2. Five designs to choose from

    Five real ideas of the same site; you keep the one you like.

    See it: Kate's five designs

  3. Your files, on your own GitHub

    Plain files you hold and can move to any host. No platform subscription.

    See it: Kate's repository

  4. Changes by talking to your AI

    Tell your AI what to change; the site says how, for you and for it.

    See it: Kate's "Use with your AI"

  5. A start page and your own numbers

    How to take it over, and an admin page with what has changed.

    See it: Kate's admin page
